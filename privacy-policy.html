Important: We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data with care. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Service.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you register and use our Service, we collect the following personal information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number
  • Authentication Data: Encrypted password, authentication tokens
  • Payment Information: Billing details processed securely through Razorpay (we do not store card numbers)

1.2 Voter Data

The Service extracts publicly available voter information from the Kerala State Election Commission portal, including:

  • Voter names and serial numbers
  • Electoral card (EPIC) numbers
  • House names and numbers
  • Guardian/relative names
  • Polling station assignments
  • Ward and local body information

Important: This voter data is public information already available on the Kerala SEC portal. We extract it based on your input and provide it to you in an organized format.

1.3 Usage Information

We automatically collect certain information about your use of the Service:

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, device type
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, time spent on Service
  • Order History: Orders created, payment status, download history
  • Technical Data: Error logs, performance metrics

1.4 Uploaded Content

We store content you upload to customize your voter slips:

  • Political party symbols/logos
  • Custom text and descriptions
  • Symbol names in English and Malayalam

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Service Delivery

We use your information to:

  • Process your orders and extract voter data
  • Generate customized voter slips with your symbols
  • Process payments securely
  • Provide customer support
  • Send order confirmations and updates

2.2 Service Improvement

We analyze usage data to:

  • Improve Service functionality and performance
  • Fix technical issues and bugs
  • Develop new features based on user needs
  • Optimize user experience

2.3 Communication

We may use your email and phone number to:

  • Send order notifications and updates
  • Provide customer support responses
  • Send important Service announcements
  • Notify you of changes to policies or terms

Note: We do not send promotional or marketing emails without your explicit consent.

2.4 Legal Compliance

We may use or disclose your information to:

  • Comply with legal obligations and court orders
  • Enforce our Terms and Conditions
  • Protect our rights and property
  • Prevent fraud and abuse of the Service

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in MongoDB databases with the following protections:

  • Encryption: Passwords are encrypted using bcrypt
  • Secure Authentication: JWT tokens for session management
  • Access Control: Role-based access (user/admin)
  • Regular Backups: Automated database backups

3.2 Payment Security

All payment processing is handled by Razorpay, a PCI DSS compliant payment gateway:

  • We do not store or have access to your card details
  • Payment data is encrypted during transmission
  • Transactions are processed over secure HTTPS connections

3.3 File Storage

Generated PDFs and uploaded symbols are stored with appropriate security:

  • Preview PDFs: Temporary storage (auto-deleted after 5 minutes)
  • Permanent PDFs: Stored for 30 days after order completion
  • Uploaded Symbols: Stored securely in our server filesystem
  • Access Control: Only the order owner can download their PDFs

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 Third-Party Services

We share limited data with the following third-party services:

  • Razorpay: Payment processing (billing information only)
  • Kerala SEC Portal: Automated extraction of public voter data

4.2 We Do NOT Share Data With:

  • Advertisers or marketing companies
  • Data brokers or analytics firms
  • Political parties or campaigns (except your own orders)
  • Any third party for commercial purposes

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, including:

  • Court orders or subpoenas
  • Election Commission investigations
  • Law enforcement requests
  • Protection of legal rights

5. Data Retention

5.1 Retention Periods

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active
  • Order History: Retained for 1 year after order completion
  • Permanent PDFs: Deleted 30 days after order completion
  • Preview PDFs: Auto-deleted after 5 minutes
  • Payment Records: Retained for 7 years (tax compliance)
  • Usage Logs: Retained for 90 days

5.2 Account Deletion

You may request account deletion by contacting us. Upon deletion:

  • Your account and personal data will be permanently deleted
  • Order history will be anonymized (for record-keeping)
  • Generated PDFs will be deleted
  • Payment records will be retained for legal compliance (7 years)

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Access and Correction

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data stored in our system
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Update your account information at any time

6.2 Data Portability

You can download your order history and generated PDFs from your dashboard at any time.

6.3 Opt-Out

You can opt out of:

  • Non-essential email communications (unsubscribe link provided)
  • Optional features like analytics and performance monitoring

6.4 Deletion Requests

You can request deletion of:

  • Specific orders and their associated data
  • Your entire account and all associated data

7. Cookies and Tracking

7.1 Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Authentication tokens (required for login)
  • Preference Cookies: User settings and customizations
  • Security Cookies: Session management and CSRF protection

7.2 We Do NOT Use

  • Third-party advertising cookies
  • Social media tracking pixels
  • Cross-site tracking mechanisms

8. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data is stored and processed in India. If you access our Service from outside India, please be aware that your data will be transferred to and processed in India, subject to Indian data protection laws.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page
  • Sending an email notification to registered users
  • Displaying a prominent notice on our Service

Your continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

11. Contact Information

For privacy-related questions, concerns, or requests, please contact us:

  • Email: Available on our Contact Page
  • Response Time: We aim to respond within 48 hours

12. Compliance

This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with:

  • Information Technology Act, 2000 (India)
  • Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices) Rules, 2011
  • Kerala State data protection regulations
  • General data protection best practices

Your Privacy Matters: We are committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy. If you have any concerns or questions about how we handle your data, please don't hesitate to contact us.
