Important Notice: This is a digital service. We do not ship physical products. All election party slips are delivered electronically as downloadable PDF files.

1. Nature of Service - Digital Delivery

EasySlip is a completely digital platform. Upon successful payment, you will receive:

  • Digital PDF Files: Professionally formatted election party slips in PDF format
  • Instant Access: Download links available immediately after PDF generation
  • No Physical Shipping: No physical products will be shipped to your address

2. Delivery Timeline

2.1 Standard Delivery Process

Step 1: Order Creation (Immediate)

After extracting voter data and customizing your slips, you create an order. A preview of the first 10 election party slips is generated instantly.

Step 2: Payment Processing (1-2 minutes)

Complete payment securely through Razorpay. Payment confirmation is received within 1-2 minutes.

Step 3: PDF Generation (Varies by Size)

Full PDF generation begins immediately after payment confirmation:

  • Small orders (1-100 voters): 1-3 minutes
  • Medium orders (101-500 voters): 3-10 minutes
  • Large orders (501-1000 voters): 10-20 minutes
  • Very large orders (1000+ voters): 20-50 minutes

Step 4: Download Ready (Instant)

Once PDF generation is complete, the download link becomes available in your dashboard. You receive an email notification (if enabled).

2.2 Background Processing

Smart PDF Generation: To provide a better experience, full PDF generation begins automatically when you preview your order. By the time you complete payment, your PDF is often already ready for download!

3. Delivery Method

3.1 Dashboard Download

The primary delivery method is through your user dashboard:

  • Log in to your account at dashboard
  • Navigate to "My Orders" section
  • Find your completed order
  • Click the "Download PDF" button
  • PDF will download directly to your device

3.2 Direct Download Link

After successful payment, you are redirected to a success page with a direct download button for immediate access.

3.3 Email Notification (Optional)

If email notifications are enabled in your account settings, you will receive an email when your PDF is ready. The email contains a secure download link.

4. File Format and Specifications

4.1 PDF Format

  • Format: Adobe PDF (Portable Document Format)
  • Page Size: A4 (210mm × 297mm)
  • Orientation: Portrait
  • Print-Ready: High-quality, suitable for professional printing

4.2 Slip Layout

  • Slips Per Page: 5 or 6 (based on your selection)
  • Perforation Guides: Dotted lines for easy cutting
  • Fonts: Noto Sans Malayalam (embedded in PDF)
  • Symbols: High-resolution, embedded in PDF

4.3 File Size

File size varies based on the number of voters:

  • 100 voters: ~2-4 MB
  • 500 voters: ~10-15 MB
  • 1000 voters: ~20-30 MB

5. Download Availability

5.1 Immediate Access

Once generated, PDFs are available for immediate download with no restrictions on the number of downloads.

5.2 Storage Period

  • Active Storage: 30 days from order completion
  • Recommendation: Download and save your PDF immediately after payment
  • Multiple Downloads: You can download the same PDF multiple times within the 30-day period

5.3 After Storage Period

After 30 days, PDFs are automatically deleted from our servers to manage storage. If you need the PDF again after this period, you may need to create a new order.

6. System Requirements for Download

6.1 Internet Connection

  • Recommended: Stable broadband connection (2 Mbps or higher)
  • Minimum: 1 Mbps for small to medium files
  • Note: Large orders (1000+ voters) may require faster connections

6.2 Browser Compatibility

  • Google Chrome (recommended)
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Safari (macOS/iOS)
  • Mobile browsers supported

6.3 PDF Viewing

To view and print the downloaded PDF, you need:

  • Adobe Acrobat Reader (free)
  • Or any modern PDF viewer
  • Built-in browser PDF viewers are supported

7. Download Issues and Support

7.1 Common Issues

PDF Generation Taking Too Long:

  • Large orders (1000+ voters) may take up to 50 minutes
  • Check the "PDF Status" indicator in your dashboard
  • Refresh the page to update status

Download Failed or Interrupted:

  • Check your internet connection
  • Try downloading again (multiple attempts allowed)
  • Use a different browser if the issue persists
  • Disable browser extensions that may block downloads

Cannot Find Download Link:

  • Log in to your dashboard
  • Go to "My Orders"
  • Look for orders with "Completed" status
  • Click "Download PDF" button

7.2 Technical Support

If you experience issues with downloading your PDF:

  • Check your email for the download link (if notifications are enabled)
  • Clear your browser cache and cookies
  • Try using a different device or browser
  • Contact our support team through the Contact Page

8. Printing Your Election Party Slips

8.1 Printing Recommendations

  • Paper: A4 size, 80-100 GSM white paper
  • Printer: Laser or inkjet printer (color or black & white)
  • Settings: Actual size (100%), no scaling
  • Orientation: Portrait
  • Quality: High or Best quality for symbols

8.2 Cutting the Slips

  • Use the dotted lines as guides
  • Use a paper cutter or scissors
  • Each page contains 5 or 6 individual slips

9. No Physical Shipping

Important:

  • We do NOT offer physical printing or shipping services
  • You are responsible for printing the PDF files yourself
  • No shipping address is required during checkout
  • No courier or postal services are involved

10. Refund and Re-Delivery

For information about refunds and re-delivery in case of technical issues, please refer to our Cancellation and Refunds Policy.

11. Data Security During Delivery

11.1 Secure Download

  • All downloads are served over HTTPS (encrypted connection)
  • Download links are authenticated (require login)
  • Only the order owner can download their PDF

11.2 Privacy Protection

  • Voter data in PDFs is public information (Kerala SEC)
  • Your account information remains private
  • Download history is visible only to you

12. Customer Support

For assistance with download or delivery issues:

  • Email Support: Available through our Contact Page
  • Response Time: Within 24-48 hours
  • Operating Hours: Monday to Saturday, 9 AM - 6 PM IST

13. Changes to Delivery Policy

We may update this Shipping Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
